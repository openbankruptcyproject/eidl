EIDL in Chapter 7

Chapter 7 is the most common path for individual borrowers with a personal-guaranteed EIDL. The loan is treated as general unsecured debt and discharged at the conclusion of the case unless an exception applies.

What happens to the EIDL in Chapter 7

  1. The SBA receives notice of the bankruptcy filing and the automatic stay applies (11 U.S.C. § 362). Treasury Offset Program (TOP) tax-refund seizures must stop.
  2. The SBA may file a proof of claim. The claim is general unsecured (the SBA's collateral, if any, is on business assets — not yours).
  3. The trustee administers any non-exempt assets. Most consumer Chapter 7 cases are no-asset cases.
  4. The SBA may file an adversary proceeding under § 523(a)(2)/(4)/(6) if fraud is alleged. Deadline is 60 days after the § 341 meeting.
  5. If no AP is filed (or the SBA loses), the discharge order eliminates your personal liability on the EIDL.

The fraud-AP risk window

The SBA has 60 days after your § 341 meeting of creditors to file an adversary proceeding objecting to discharge of the EIDL. If you have an OIG investigation underway, expect an AP. If you don't, the SBA generally does not file unless something jumps out in the schedules.

What raises the risk of an AP:

  • OIG investigation already opened (you typically know — subpoena, civil investigative demand, or interview request)
  • EIDL on a business that doesn't appear to have existed (no tax filings, no payroll, no vendors)
  • Significant transfers of loan proceeds to insiders or to non-business uses (vehicles, real estate)
  • Loan amount disproportionate to documented revenue

Business-entity Chapter 7 vs individual Chapter 7

If the EIDL was a no-PG loan to an LLC or corporation, the entity itself can file Chapter 7 to liquidate. The entity does not receive a discharge (11 U.S.C. § 727(a)(1)). Liquidation simply distributes assets and the remaining debt becomes uncollectable as a practical matter (the entity ceases to exist).

For an individual with a PG-backed EIDL, individual Chapter 7 is what discharges the personal liability.
